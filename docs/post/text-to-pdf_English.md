## Tips for a PDF that looks good

- **Use 12 pt text for letters and notes.** With Normal line spacing and Normal margins, it's easy to read.
- **Add a title.** It shows at the top of the PDF and helps people know what the file is about.
- **Use headings.** Break long text into small parts with Heading 1 and Heading 2.
- **Use Justified alignment for long paragraphs.** It makes both edges of the text line up neatly.
- **Always check the Preview.** It shows the real pages before you download.
- **Skip emoji.** Emoji and a few rare symbols show up as a question mark in the PDF.
