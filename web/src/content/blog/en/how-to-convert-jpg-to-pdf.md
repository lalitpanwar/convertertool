## Tips to get a clear PDF

- **Take the photo in good light.** Daylight works best. Keep the paper flat so the words are easy to read.
- **Cut off the extra parts.** Use your phone's edit button to crop out the table, floor or your fingers.
- **Pick A4 for documents.** Certificates, ID cards and forms look right and print well on A4.
- **Check the order.** For an ID card, put the front side first and the back side second.
- **Add all photos together.** This way you get one PDF with many pages, not many small files.
- **Watch the file size.** Some forms only take small files, like 200 KB or 1 MB. If your PDF is too big, compress it after you make it.

## Other ways to do it (without a website)

Your computer or phone can also make a PDF on its own. It just takes a few more clicks.

**On Windows**

1. Select your photos, right-click and choose **Print**.
2. In the printer list, pick **Microsoft Print to PDF**.
3. Click **Print**, give the file a name and save it.

**On Mac**

1. Open your photos in **Preview**.
2. Go to **File**, then **Print**.
3. Click **PDF** and choose **Save as PDF**.

**On iPhone**

1. Open the photo and tap the **Share** button.
2. Tap **Print**, then spread two fingers on the preview to zoom in.
3. Tap **Share** again and choose **Save to Files**.

**On Android**

1. Open the photo, tap the three dots and choose **Print**.
2. Pick **Save as PDF** as the printer.
3. Tap the PDF button and save.

These ways work fine for one photo. But if you have many photos, the online tool is quicker and lets you change the order easily.
